# Local analyzer design

## Authority

Jeremy approved the localhost frontend plan on 2026-11-16. Reference: https://jeremyevan.vercel.app/. Browser inspection found white-and-black editorial composition, Instrument Serif mixed with bold Google Sans Flex, IBM Plex Mono labels, thin rules and broad spacing.

## Direction

Operate mode: enter a job and optional profile, then inspect an evidence-backed report. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Build directly from the approved interface plan, without generated imagery.

- White and black preserve the portfolio identity; restrained status colors distinguish comparison outcomes and always have text labels.
- Mixed serif and bold sans headings provide the identifying gesture. Monospace labels distinguish metadata from report prose and recall the reference.
- A wide editorial title leads directly into the workspace. Desktop input/results regions and stacked mobile sections serve the reading/editing task.
- Thin rules organize dense evidence without a repeated card grid. Whitespace separates tasks and report sections; controls stay near their content.
- Disclosure rows keep evidence available without overwhelming the first scan. Required/preferred counts communicate actual outcomes rather than a speculative score.
- Short focus/hover and result transitions explain interactions. No pinned scrolling, marquee, artificial progress, decorative illustration or marketing hero.
- Controls have readable contrast and keyboard focus. Mobile headings remain compact, controls wrap and long source quotes break safely.
